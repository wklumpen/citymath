The math doesn't work

Calgary needs $54 billion of infrastructure over the next ten years, but has funded only half of it. That leaves us with a $27 billion shortfall.

Here we explore just one piece of that budget: the roads. Following the City's own recommended pavement plan, we would use up most of the mobility budget just on road repair, before addressing a single sidewalk, streetlight or bike lane.

Sources: City of Calgary, 10-year Capital Infrastructure Plan, May 2026, p. 2 · Report to Executive Committee EC2026-0159, p. 2 · Strong Towns Calgary, "Calgary, we are in a predicament"
