The same measure, added up by when each area was built. Parks, undeveloped land and special districts such as the airport are left out here and shown in grey on the map.

What is countedRoad pavement only. Pipes, transit, parks, police and everything else the city pays for are not in this picture.
Where the tax number comes fromThe 2025 assessment roll times the 2025 municipal tax rate. Hospitals, schools, parks and other exempt property are removed. The total lands within about 1% of the City's reported figure.
Where the road number comes fromStreet lengths from OpenStreetMap, widths measured on a sample of residential streets in each era of neighbourhood, and rebuild costs of $300 to $350 per square metre.
